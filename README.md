### Paris Moschovakos

Paris Moschovakos is Head of Detector Controls for the ATLAS experiment at CERN and a member of the ISO C++ Standardization Committee (WG21), where he represents the safety-systems practitioner's voice. He builds and operates safety-critical distributed control systems at extreme scale: hundreds of servers and thousands of industrial I/O endpoints running hardware safety interlocks around the clock. He is the lead developer of [quasar](https://github.com/quasar-team/quasar), the open-source framework behind 20+ C++ OPC UA server projects, and shipped the first production deployment of C++26 Contracts in a live safety-critical system. He writes and speaks about what extreme-scale operations teach about building systems that actually work.
